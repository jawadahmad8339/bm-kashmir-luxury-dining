## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the timeless opulence and hospitality of royal Kashmiri and Mughlai culinary traditions, curated for a modern luxury dining presence in Lahore. It communicates majestic lineage, slow-cooked craftsmanship, warmth, and culinary prestige.

The aesthetic philosophy draws on **heritage luxury and tactile editorial refinement**:
- **Opulence with Restraint:** Deep jewel tones and brushed metallic gold trims frame generous linen-hued surfaces without visual clutter.
- **Kashmiri Artistry:** Subtle geometric framing and delicate hairline fillets evoke classic *sozni* embroidery, *naqashi* wood carvings, and *jali* latticework.
- **Warm Culinary Gastronomy:** Imagery and color treatment spotlight the rich aromatic depths of saffron, Kashmiri chili, cardamom, and slow-braised delicacies.
- **Emotional Tone:** Prestigious, warm, ceremonial, and deeply hospitable.

## Colors

The palette balances royal deep-tone grandeur with warm, welcoming dining hospitality.

- **Primary (`#3B1218` - Imperial Rogan Burgundy):** A rich, slow-simmered burgundy inspired by deep Kashmiri rogan josh gravies and royal velvet banquettes. Used for dominant headings, primary action buttons, ceremonial hero banners, and high-impact containers.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37` - Zaffran Antique Gold):** Inspired by pure saffron threads and hand-beaten brass *samovars*. Used for hairline borders, micro-badges, star ratings, metallic decorative filigrees, and focal interactive accents.
- **Tertiary (`#0D281E` - Midnight Emerald):** A regal deep pine-emerald recalling Chinar tree leaves and Mughal pavilion gardens. Used for vegetarian markers, signature badges, reserve indicators, and specialized highlight cards.
- **Neutral Light (`#FAF7F2` - Warm Cream Silk):** An untreated linen and warm ivory canvas that eliminates sterile digital white, providing an appetizing, paper-like luxury editorial backdrop.
- **Body & Text Neutral (`#1C1412` - Roasted Espresso):** Deep charcoal-espresso ensuring high legibility for menus, descriptions, prices, and functional UI without the harshness of pitch black.
- **Amber Accent (`#C67D28` - Charred Honey Amber):** Used sparingly for urgent highlights, delivery estimations, live availability, and status tags.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs high-society editorial elegance with effortless transactional clarity.

- **Editorial Elegance (Playfair Display):** Reserved for dish names, ceremonial storytelling, section headings, and quotes. Italics may be utilized for culinary descriptors (e.g., *Slow-dum cooked lamb shanks in saffron broth*).
- **Modern Utilitarian Clarity (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Deployed for all menu item pricing, calorie markers, checkout elements, reservation workflows, filter tabs, and metadata chips.
- **Monetary & Currency Conventions:** Currency prefixes (e.g., `PKR`) are formatted using `label-sm` in semi-bold uppercase, paired with tabular numeral figures from `price-display`.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive layout system provides deliberate white space, framed balance, and generous breathing room worthy of high-end dining.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 768px` (4 columns, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`).
  - Tablet: `768px - 1199px` (8 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`).
  - Desktop: `≥ 1200px` (12 columns, max-width `1320px`, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`).
- **Vertical Rhythm:** 
  - Standard section transitions: `space-xl` multiplied dynamically (64px to 96px).
  - Food menu card content internal padding: `space-lg` (24px).
  - List item stacks and micro elements: `space-sm` (8px) to `space-md` (16px).
- **Reflow Rules:** Food menu cards render in 3 columns on desktop, 2 columns on tablet, and a single vertical stack on mobile with full-bleed photography.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through warm ambient shadows, metallic hairline borders, and layered parchment surfaces.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Base Surface:* Warm cream silk (`#FAF7F2`).
  - *Elevated Cards:* Pure alabaster parchment (`#FFFDFB`) layered over the warm base.
  - *Sunken Insets / Quantity Toggles:* Delicate warm sandstone tint (`#F1EBE1`).
- **Borders & Insets:**
  - Cards feature a 1px structural hairline outline (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.28)`) that intensifies on hover (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.7)`).
- **Ambient Shadow Spectrum:**
  - *Level 1 (Menu Cards & Chips):* `0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(59, 18, 24, 0.05), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(28, 20, 18, 0.03)`
  - *Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Dropdowns):* `0px 12px 28px -4px rgba(59, 18, 24, 0.08), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`
  - *Level 3 (Sticky Booking Bar & Royal Modals):* `0px 20px 48px -8px rgba(42, 8, 12, 0.22), 0px 0px 0px 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`

## Shapes

The design uses **Soft (Level 1)** geometry to evoke bespoke leather-bound menu folios, framed Mughal miniatures, and brass tableware.

- **Default Corners:** Core inputs, menu cards, and standard action buttons feature subtle `0.25rem` (4px) corner rounding.
- **Embellished Elements (`rounded-lg` - 8px):** Modal sheets, dish hero preview frames, and tasting flight containers.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Operational badges, status tags (e.g., *Open 24 Hours*), and floating quick-order counters strictly utilize full capsule rounding to distinguish utility elements from content containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Imperial CTA:** Solid Burgundy (`#3B1218`) fill, Antique Gold (`#D4AF37`) text, 1px border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`). `height: 48px`, font `label-lg`, uppercase with slight tracking (`0.06em`). Hover: Deep burgundy darkens with elevated gold luminescence shadow.
- **Secondary Royal Outline:** Transparent fill, Espresso (`#1C1412`) text, 1.5px border (`#D4AF37`). Hover: Background shifts to light saffron wash (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`).
- **Text CTA:** Espresso text with continuous hairline gold underline and right-aligned arrow (`→`).

### Heritage Status Badges & Chips
- **Royal Metadata Chips:** Pill-shaped, subtle champagne tint (`#F3E5AB`/30% opacity), thin gold border (`#D4AF37`), dark burgundy text. Used for "Open 24 Hours", "Family Friendly Dining", and "4.8 ★ Highly Rated".
- **Dietary Identifiers:** 
  - *Vegetarian:* 14px square frame in Midnight Emerald (`#0D281E`) housing a centered solid green circle.
  - *Wazwan / Chef's Royal Specialty:* Antique gold badge with subtle crown/paisley insignia.

### Dish Cards & Menu Items
- **Card Anatomy:** Off-white parchment background, 1px gold border, 4px corner radius.
- **Imagery:** 4:3 high-definition food photography with a subtle warm vignette.
- **Card Content:** Playfair Display dish title, italicized provenance note, bold Plus Jakarta Sans price (`PKR 2,450`), and an interactive "+ Add to Order" or "Customise Wazwan" button.

### Floating Booking & Order Bar
- Persistent bottom dock on mobile and floating anchored bar on desktop.
- Rich Burgundy canvas (`#2A080C`) with a polished top gold border. Houses table reservation selector, guest count dropdown, and instantaneous "Reserve Royal Table" CTA.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background in Warm Cream Silk (`#FAF7F2`) with bottom-bordered or 1px framed `#D4AF37` hairline.
- Focus state: Outline sharpens to pure gold with a soft inner gold aura. Floating label transitions to Playfair Display italic.

### Testimonial & Heritage Quote Sliders
- Center-aligned card styled as an illuminated manuscript. Accompanied by twin quotation filigrees in muted antique gold and italicized body copy (`body-lg`).
